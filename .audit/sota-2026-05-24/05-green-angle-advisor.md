# Green-Angle Advisor — SOTA 2026-05-24

Veteran-expert green opportunity scanner: given ANY project
description (e.g. "we're building a railway from Dar es Salaam to
Dodoma"), surfaces sustainability angles, ESG-linked financing,
regulatory grant alignment, partnership opportunities, carbon-credit
participation, and jurisdictional alignment.

Reasons at the level of a 25-year sustainability consultant —
recognises 30+ canonical opportunity patterns + composes them per
project shape, location, and scale.

---

## 1. Project-type taxonomy (14 categories)

Each project type carries characteristic environmental footprints,
permitting paths, and "natural" green angles. The advisor classifies
free-form prose into one or more of these (a corridor project is
both `infrastructure-rail` and `infrastructure-highway` if mixed).

| Code                          | Examples                                              | Hot green angles                                                                              |
|-------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `residential`                 | Apartments, single-family, mixed-use housing          | Solar PV, rainwater, IFC EDGE, embodied-carbon timber, EV charging                            |
| `commercial-office`           | CBD towers, corporate HQ                              | LEED/EDGE, district cooling, on-site PV, smart HVAC, end-of-trip facilities                   |
| `retail`                      | Shopping centres, big-box, high-street                | PV roof canopies, EV charging hubs, refrigerant transition (HFC → CO₂)                        |
| `hospitality`                 | Hotels, resorts, lodges                               | Greywater reuse, biodiversity restoration on grounds, eco-tourism carbon offsets              |
| `industrial`                  | Factories, warehouses, light manufacturing            | Process-heat electrification, waste-heat capture, circular feedstock, on-site PV              |
| `infrastructure-rail`         | Heavy rail, light rail, BRT, SGR, metro               | Corridor solar, modal-shift carbon credit, biodiversity over/under-passes, station SuDS       |
| `infrastructure-port`         | Seaports, container terminals, river ports            | Shore power, blue-carbon mangrove restoration, dredge-spoil habitat creation                  |
| `infrastructure-airport`      | New runways, terminals, MRO facilities                | SAF demand aggregation, solar-roof terminals, taxi-out electrification                        |
| `infrastructure-highway`      | Toll roads, expressways, bypasses                     | Wildlife crossings (BNG), solar median, EV-charging corridor, in-road inductive               |
| `mining`                      | Open-pit, underground, mineral processing             | Mine-pit hydro storage, progressive rehabilitation, BNG, biodiversity offsets, fugitive CH₄    |
| `energy`                      | Power gen (gas/coal/PV/wind/geo), T&D, storage        | Co-located PV+BESS, methane abatement, grid-services credits, hydrogen co-production         |
| `agriculture`                 | Plantations, irrigation schemes, agroforestry         | Regenerative ag, biochar (VM0044/0048), VCS reforestation, riparian buffers                   |
| `water`                       | Reservoirs, treatment, irrigation, desal              | Watershed PES, blue-carbon recharge, sludge-to-biogas, brine valorisation                     |
| `telecom`                     | Cell towers, fibre, data centres                      | DC liquid cooling, PUE optimisation, edge-PV/diesel hybrid, waste-heat district               |

Sources: IFC Performance Standards 1-8, IDB Environmental Safeguards,
Equator Principles 5 (EP5, 2025 refresh), AfDB Integrated Safeguards
System (ISS), EBRD Environmental and Social Policy (2024).

### 3.2 Gold Standard (GS, 2026 active)

- **GS LUF** — Land-Use & Forestry: AR-ACM0003, AR-AMS0007
- **GS Energy Efficiency** — cookstoves, water-purification
- **GS Renewable Energy** — small-scale grid PV/wind/hydro
- **GS for Global Goals** — SDG-aligned methodology suite

### 3.3 Article 6.4 PACM (Paris Agreement Crediting Mechanism)

Replaces CDM. Methodologies adopted at CMA.6 (Baku, Nov 2024) and
expanded at CMA.7 (Belém, Nov 2025). Eligible project types:

- Removals (afforestation, biochar, BECCS, DAC + storage)
- Avoidance (avoided deforestation in low-deforestation jurisdictions)
- Reductions (renewable energy, energy efficiency, methane abatement)

Includes 5 % share-of-proceeds for the Adaptation Fund + 2 % overall
mitigation contribution (cancelled credits).

### 3.4 Voluntary registries / standards

- **Climate Action Reserve (CAR)** — US-focused, rice, dairy
- **American Carbon Registry (ACR)** — forest, ARB-CORC, advanced
  refrigerants
- **Plan Vivo** — smallholder community AR/IFM
- **Puro.earth** — durable removals (biochar, enhanced weathering,
  bio-oil sequestration), 2025+ rapidly growing

## 4. Biodiversity offsets + BNG units

### 4.1 UK Biodiversity Net Gain (BNG, mandatory Feb 2024)

10 % uplift required for most planning permissions (England). Unit
price 2026 spot: GBP 12,000 – 30,000 per biodiversity unit (BU)
depending on habitat type. Statutory credits floor at GBP 42,000 /
BU (2× lowest market price).

### 4.2 IFC Performance Standard 6 — Biodiversity Net Gain

For critical habitat impacts: net gain required (not just no net
loss). Often satisfied through ratio-based offsets (typ. 10:1 for
critical, 3:1 for natural).

### 4.3 ENCORE + TNFD-aligned biodiversity exposure

ENCORE database (Natural Capital Finance Alliance) maps sector
dependencies. Used to scope `LEAP` (Locate, Evaluate, Assess,
Prepare) under TNFD.

### 4.4 East-African biodiversity contexts

- **Kenya** — Wildlife Conservation & Management Act 2013
  (compensation funds, wildlife corridors)
- **Tanzania** — Wildlife Conservation Act 2009 (WMAs), Marine Parks
  and Reserves Act
- **Uganda** — Wildlife Act 2019 (compensation, offsets)

## 7. Co-benefits scoring

Multi-criteria scoring uses SDG alignment + 4 co-benefit dimensions:

| Dimension                | Metric                                      | Weight (default) |
|--------------------------|---------------------------------------------|------------------|
| SDG alignment            | Count of SDGs (out of 17) materially served | 0.30             |
| Jobs                     | Direct + induced FTE per USD 1 m invested   | 0.20             |
| Health (PM2.5)           | μg/m³ reduction in catchment population     | 0.20             |
| Water security           | m³/yr saved or made available               | 0.15             |
| Gender / inclusion       | % beneficiaries female + youth              | 0.15             |

Weights are tenant-tunable; default reflects Equator Principles 5 +
GCF Performance Measurement Framework (PMF) v3 (2024).

## 9. Reference "railway from A to B" canonical fixture

For a 450 km heavy-rail corridor (Dar es Salaam → Dodoma, mixed
freight + passenger, coastal start + dryland mid-corridor) the
advisor must surface AT LEAST these 5 opportunities:

1. **Corridor solar co-location** — 200+ MW potential along ROW
   easement; East-African insolation 5.5–6.5 kWh/m²/day.
2. **Land-bridge BNG** — Mikumi / Selous proximity → mandatory IFC
   PS6 critical-habitat treatment → wildlife crossings + offset
   ratio 10:1.
3. **Water reclaim / SuDS at stations** — Dar corridor has 1,300 mm/yr
   rainfall, Dodoma 600 mm/yr → rainwater + SuDS yields material
   on-site demand offset.
4. **Regenerative-ag corridor** — 1 km buffer zone agroforestry
   (cashew + sorghum + livestock rotation) yields VM0042 credits
   plus livelihood program.
5. **Modal-shift freight carbon credit** — VMR0006 baseline shifts
   tonne-km from road to rail; for 5 Mt/yr lifted from HDV trucks
   to electrified rail ≈ 200,000 tCO₂e/yr.

Bonus angles (returned but lower-ranked unless flagged):

- Shore-power at Dar Port intermodal
- Blue-carbon mangrove restoration at Dar terminal (Mbweni / Kunduchi)
- Cement substitution (Tanga CCS pilot) for tunnel + bridge segments
- Sustainability-Linked Loan with KPI = tCO₂e shifted from road

## 10. Composition strategy (advisor)

For a `ProjectDescription` we run, in order:

1. `ProjectClassifier.classify(description)` → one or more
   `ProjectTypeTag`s + scale + location + sector signals.
2. `OpportunityMatcher.match(profile)` → ranked
   `GreenOpportunity[]` from the 30+ catalog.
3. `FinancingMatcher.match(profile, opportunities)` →
   `FinancingMatch[]` ranked by fit + access.
4. `MethodologyMatcher.match(profile, opportunities)` →
   `CarbonProjectMatch[]` with eligibility + volume + value forecast.
5. `SdgAlignmentScorer + CoBenefitsScorer` → `ImpactScore`.
6. `OpportunityPrioritizer.rank()` → MCDA (IRR vs. ESG vs. urgency,
   weights tenant-tunable).
7. `VeteranExpertReport.compose()` → narrative + tables; **optional**
   LLM-synthesis layer via injected `MultiLLMSynthesizer` port for
   the final narrative.

Every matcher is **PURE** (deterministic for fixed inputs). LLM
calls only ever happen through the synthesizer port.
