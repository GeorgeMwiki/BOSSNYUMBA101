# Project boundary

**This repository is BOSSNYUMBA only.**

**BossNyumba is an AI-native real estate operating system. Mr. Mwikila
is its brain layer.**

Mr. Mwikila is the brain layer within BossNyumba — an AI-native real
estate operating system. He drafts, hedges, supervises, files. The
owner / landlord / agent signs once. The product evolves
independently, with its own roadmap, schema, OpenAPI surface, apps,
juniors, regulator pack, marketing surface, and CI pipeline.

BossNyumba covers East African real estate end-to-end: leases, rent,
maintenance, treasury, compliance, marketplace, holdings, subsidiaries,
ancillary businesses, family office, succession, and the full asset
register. **Real estate domain only — never mining.**

- **BOSSNYUMBA** = this repo. AI-native real estate operating system
  for East Africa (owners, admins, estate managers, tenants).
- **Pongezi** = a **separate project**. Different codebase, different
  product. Not in this repo.

Do **not**:
- Refer to Pongezi in BOSSNYUMBA code, docs, or config.
- Copy or assume code/docs from Pongezi unless explicitly migrating.
- Conflate the two projects in architecture, env, or naming.
- Refer to mining, mineral licences, royalty, PCCB, or any
  mining-domain concept in BossNyumba code, docs, or marketing copy —
  that belongs to a different product entirely.

All work in this repo applies to BOSSNYUMBA only.
